## Imagery — original work

All imagery on the site is **original generative artwork made for this project**. Nothing is hotlinked or downloaded from a third party.

Each image is a thermal-camera-style render of a posed figure. A WebGL fragment shader (`src/lib/thermal/shader.ts`) builds the figure from signed-distance shapes and maps temperature to an ember colour ramp. The static images are rendered offline with headless Chromium (`npm run art`). The hero canvas runs the same shader live.

### Why there are no stock photos

The brief called for Unsplash, Pexels, Mixkit and Coverr media. This build environment's network policy blocks those hosts (`images.unsplash.com`, `images.pexels.com`, `videos.pexels.com`, `assets.mixkit.co`, `coverr.co`). The brief's fallback applies, so the site uses original art instead of placeholders. There are no broken images and no hotlinks.

To swap in photography later:

1. Allow those hosts in the environment.
2. Drop the files into `public/`.
3. Point the `art` paths in `src/content/site.ts` at them.

Every image goes through `next/image`, which serves AVIF/WebP at the right sizes.
